15-Minute Website Audit Checklist

Use this before buying ads or redesigning. Most lead leaks are visible in one focused pass.

First screen clarity

  • Can a stranger tell what you do in five seconds?
  • Is there one obvious next action?
  • Is the phone/contact action visible before scrolling?

Trust proof

  • Do reviews, photos, credentials, or examples appear near the offer?
  • Does the site show a real location/service area?
  • Are policies, contact details, and business identity easy to verify?

Local SEO basics

  • Is the business name, phone, and service area consistent?
  • Do service pages answer buyer questions instead of repeating generic copy?
  • Are Google Business Profile categories, photos, and reviews current?

Mobile speed

  • Do large images slow the first screen?
  • Do popups or scripts block the main action?
  • Can the form be completed easily on a phone?

Measurement

  • Are calls, forms, and booking clicks tracked?
  • Is there a simple weekly lead log?
  • Can you identify which page generated each inquiry?
